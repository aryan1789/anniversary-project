❓ How To Play

Getting around

WASD or arrow keys to walk, Space to jump, drag the mouse to look around, and E to interact with glowing things.

The goal

Explore each room, talk to everything that glows, collect memories, solve the riddle, and make your way to the treasure at the end.

Make it yours

🎨 Customize your look · 🛠️ Build mode lets you decorate each room with trees, lights, balloons and more — click a placed item to move, rotate or delete it · 💃 Emotes to wave, dance or cheer · 🔊 Mutes the music and sound.

Your progress and anything you build are saved automatically on this device.

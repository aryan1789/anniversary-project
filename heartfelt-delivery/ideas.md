# Anniversary Gift: Software Ideas

She likes LEGO, Sims and Roblox. Timeline: about one month (no rush on the exact date).

## 1. "Our Year" Sims-style life sim
- Small browser game with a top-down or isometric house and your two characters.
- Each room holds a real memory. Click the couch to see a photo and a note.
- Sims touches: mood bar, "wants" panel, plumbob over her head.
- Ends with an "aspiration complete" screen and the anniversary message.
- Stack: Phaser or Canvas with React, deployed to Vercel, opens on her phone.

## 2. Roblox obby / world made for her (CHOSEN DIRECTION)
- Built in Roblox Studio (Lua), played in an app she already uses.
- Each stage is a memory: first date, favourite places, inside jokes.
- Checkpoints as photo signs, final stage is the reveal.
- Treasure-hunt variant: she finds an object in the world and it gives her the next clue.
- More effort to learn Roblox Studio, but more meaningful because it is her platform.

## 3. LEGO-brick memory mosaic / timeline
- Photo of the two of you turned into a brick mosaic with a real parts list (Bricklink Studio is free).
- Or a web page where each month is a LEGO-style tile that flips to show a memory.
- Pairs well with a real LEGO set as the physical gift.

## 4. Small cozy game / interactive story
- "Would you rather / trivia about us" game that unlocks the next clue for a physical gift or date.

## Decision
Going with #2 as a treasure hunt: she finds something in the Roblox world and it gives her clues that lead onward.
